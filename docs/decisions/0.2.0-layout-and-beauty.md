# 0.2.0: layout, reading model and beauty checks

Status: accepted by the user on 2026-09-28 after a screenshot review of the
0.1.0 demos. Builds on `0.2.0-chrome-and-arrival.md`.

## Problems seen in the 0.1.0 screenshots
1. Every chapter was a text box laid over a full-bleed 3D backdrop ("a site on
   a wallpaper"): heavy dark shades, grey and cream cards across the subject.
2. The camera did not know where the text was: subjects cropped by the top
   edge, cut by panels, or parked behind paragraphs.
3. The same chapter structure everywhere (eyebrow, heading, meta, paragraph),
   centred long paragraphs, small scale contrast.
4. Muddy, low-contrast frames (a dark forest) and crude hero props (flat
   extruded icons, a blocky shelter).

## Decisions
- **Reading model chosen by content length** (`brief.reading`).
- **A composition vocabulary per chapter** instead of one template, with no
  identical neighbours.
- **The kit tells the scene where the text is** (`ctx.layout`), so the camera
  frames the subject in the free space. No boxes by default.
- **Magazine typography:** strong scale contrast, narrow left-aligned columns,
  no centred paragraphs.
- **Realism in a controlled set:** materials and light stay physically based,
  but worlds are bounded sets (room, table, diorama, one object on a backdrop)
  rather than open landscapes. See `rendering.md`.
- **Automatic beauty checks:** framing (overlap, crop), light (dark or flat
  frames), repetition, and crude hero objects.

## `brief.reading`
| Value | Behaviour | Default when |
|---|---|---|
| `interleave` | Long chapters sit on an opaque **paper** surface in the page flow; between them the world takes the full screen with short text. While paper fully covers the viewport the runtime skips rendering (updates continue). | total words > 900 (or > 3,600 Thai characters), or any chapter > 180 words (720 Thai characters) |
| `overlay` | Text sits directly over the world with no box. Legibility comes from a **quiet zone**: the camera finish darkens, softens and desaturates the frame only under the text column (strength from `brief.look.quiet`, default 0.6). | otherwise |
| `beside` | Split screen: the world is sticky on one side (desktop 50–58% width; mobile the top 52svh, above the page, with the text scrolling up under it), text scrolls on the other. No overlap by construction. | never by default; the director picks it for spec-like or comparison content |

The director may override the default with a reason in `chrome.reasons.reading`
(the key lives at `brief.reading`; the reason sits with the other reasons).

## `chapters[i].composition` and `chapters[i].side`
Replaces `layout` and `panel`, which the scaffold rejects with a migration hint
(`hero` → `opening`, `breath` → `interlude`, long text → `paper`, `wide` with
tables → `ledger`, `left`/`right` → `side`, any `panel` → drop it).

| Composition | Use | Text rules |
|---|---|---|
| `opening` | chapter 0 only | display headline at the largest scale, lede, then the contents and sound offer from the chrome spec |
| `paper` | long reading (`interleave`) | on the paper surface; a narrow column, optional large chapter numeral in the margin |
| `spread` | medium reading | two text columns on paper with the heading spanning both (single column below 900px) |
| `ledger` | tables, long lists, specs | full measure on paper, tabular figures, hairline rules |
| `caption` | short text over or beside the world | ≤ 60 words or ≤ 240 Thai characters; placed in one corner of the free area like a museum label |
| `dossier` | one collection item | meta row, heading, body in a narrow column on `side`; the subject fills the free area |
| `interlude` | a single display line | full-bleed world; the only composition that may centre text |

`side`: `left` · `right` · `center` (center only for `opening` and
`interlude`). **No two consecutive chapters may share the same
(composition, side)**. Text over a limit is a `BriefError` telling the
director to use `paper`, `spread` or `ledger`.

## Runtime: `ctx.layout`
- `ctx.layout.free`: the current chapter's free area, the viewport minus the
  text column(s) and a margin, as `{x, y, w, h}` in CSS px plus `ndc`
  (`{x0, y0, x1, y1}`). Recomputed on chapter change, resize, font load and at
  most every 100 ms while scrolling; never per frame.
- `ctx.layout.frame(camera, target, { fill = 0.55, bleed = false })`: returns
  `{ shiftX, shiftY, distanceScale }` that centre `target` (an `Object3D`,
  `Box3` or `Sphere`) in the free area at the given fill ratio. The scene damps
  toward these values and writes the lens shift itself
  (`camera.projectionMatrix.elements[8/9]`, then inverse). Allocation-free.
- `ctx.layout.subject(target)`: the scene declares the chapter's subject. The
  runtime projects it (at most every 250 ms) for the probe.
- Quiet zone: `createPost` gains `quiet` (0..1) and reads the text rects from
  `ctx.layout` into a small uniform array (up to 3 rects, UV space, feathered
  edges). It darkens, softens and desaturates only there. Scenes without the
  post finish get a CSS fallback: a feathered `mask`-free gradient layer
  (`.ox-quiet`) positioned behind the text column.
- Paper pause: an `IntersectionObserver` marks when paper covers the viewport;
  `render()` is skipped then, `update()` still runs.

## Probe additions (for the critic)
Only when the page URL has `?ox-qa` (so visitors pay nothing):
- `probe.layout[chapter] = { subject: {x,y,w,h}, text: [{x,y,w,h}], overlap, visible }`
  where `overlap` = subject area covered by text / subject area, and
  `visible` = subject area inside the viewport / subject area.
- `probe.light[chapter] = { mean, p05, p95 }`: relative luminance of a 32×18
  downsample of the canvas taken right after a render, once per second.

## Typography (magazine)
- Scale ratio 1.333. Opening headline up to `clamp(3.4rem, 1.6rem + 6vw, 9rem)`
  (Thai: line-height ≥ 1.25 stays).
- Measure: Latin ≤ 62ch; Thai ≤ 34em. Body always left-aligned; hanging
  bullets; tables use tabular figures.
- A large chapter numeral is available to `paper` and `dossier` (`chrome.folio`
  decides its content; it is decoration and `aria-hidden`).
- Paragraphs are never centred except the `interlude` line.

## QA
Static (`qa.py`, errors):
- `Q-repeat`: consecutive chapters with the same (composition, side).
- `Q-center`: a `text-align: center` rule in `theme.css` or the base CSS that
  reaches body paragraphs, list items or table cells outside `interlude`.
- `Q-length`: `caption`, `dossier` or `interlude` text over its limit.
- `Q-hero`: `brief.scene.hero` missing, or neither `{ "source": "cc0", "id": … }`
  nor `{ "source": "procedural", "detail": [≥ 3 concrete features] }`.
Browser (critic, via `?ox-qa`), gates in `quality-bar.md`:
- `G-frame`: every chapter with a subject has `overlap ≤ 0.12` and
  `visible ≥ 0.85` (unless the chapter sets `frame: "bleed"`).
- `G-light`: every rendered chapter has `p95 − p05 ≥ 0.30` and
  `0.10 ≤ mean ≤ 0.85`, unless the chapter declares `tone: "night"` (mean
  floor 0.05) or `tone: "bright"` (ceiling 0.92).
- `D-hero`: the critic screenshots each subject and fails flat extrusions,
  untextured primitives or blocky stand-ins as the hero.

## No-JS note
`aria-busy` is added by the head script, not written into the HTML, so pages
without JavaScript never announce a permanent busy state.
