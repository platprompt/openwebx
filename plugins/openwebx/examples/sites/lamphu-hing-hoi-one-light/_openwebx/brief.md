# One Light, One Night (ไฟดวงเดียว): creative brief

**Theme:** นาฬิกากลไกในสตูดิโอถ่ายสินค้ายามค่ำ แรงบันดาลใจจากหิ่งห้อยบนต้นลำพูริมคลอง → a night product shoot of one mechanical watch, where the reader holds the only light (original concept; fictional brand from the content; no real watch brands or marks)
**Content:** product, 9 sections, th, ~693 words (~702 reading units), FAQ ×3, no images
**Form:** Stage coloured by Hours, because the content is a single subject studied part by part (dial, movement, case, straps, specs): the studio and the slab stay, the watch is turned and re-lit for each part, and the studio's light runs from first light through lights-out to one warm lamp.

## The four questions
1. **What is the content for?** Deciding whether this watch is worth 52,000 baht: how it looks in light and in the dark, what is inside, whether it fits the wrist and daily life.
2. **Where and when?** A small product studio after hours, one night shoot: a dark green sweep, a polished slate slab, one strip softbox, a cool rim strip, and a cut lamphu branch far behind holding out-of-focus firefly points.
3. **What does the reader hold?** The light. Its object is the watch, Hing Hoi. Verb: ส่องไฟ (shine): move the light, hold to charge the lume, release to see it glow.
4. **How does it end?** The softbox switches off and one warm lamp is left; the watch rests where it began, its twelve dots glowing faintly. The set returns to its first frame, changed.

## The idea
The page opens in the dark with twelve faint dots, then the key light rises as the world loads and a three-quarter shot of the watch on slate appears beside the headline. The reader moves the light: ripples on the dial shift shade, highlights slide over the polished flanks. On the dial chapter they learn to hold the light to charge the dots, and one scroll later the studio goes dark: dots and hands glow yellow-green while the fireflies on the lamphu branch behind pulse the same colour. The lights come back for the movement (flipped, rotor with lamphu leaves, balance beating at 4 Hz), the profile, the three straps, and a catalogue shot beside the spec ledger, before the shoot winds down to one warm lamp.

## Visual language
- **World / materials / light / atmosphere:** a controlled set (sweep, slab, branch prop), never an open landscape. 316L steel with polished flanks vs brushed tops, domed sapphire with a violet AR tint, ripple-stamped green-black dial, domed lume dots, tannin leather, polished slate. Key = a movable strip softbox; rim = a cool strip that never goes off; finale = one warm practical. Faint haze only inside the key cone. **One phenomenon leads:** light stored and given back (raking light, then lume).
- **Palette:** bg `#0b100e` · surface `#131b17` · paper `#111814` · ink `#ece6d4` · muted `#9aa091` · accent `#cde27a` (lume) · accent2 `#c08a52` (tannin). Contrast ink/bg = 15.4:1, muted/bg = 7.1:1.
- **Type:** display DM Serif Display 400 / body Manrope 400·600 / labels and ledger figures DM Mono 400; Thai pairing Taviraj 300·400·600 (display) + IBM Plex Sans Thai Looped 400·500 (body). Accent style: colour only (lume). No tracking on Thai. All URLs verified (HTTP 200).
- **Motion verbs:** rake, turn, sweep, breathe. Everything damped (λ ≈ 2.5); idle motion is noise-driven, never a loop.
- **Sound palette:** the movement's soft fast tick (very low), a quiet room tone, a light-switch click on chapter turns; grip/let-go answer the charge and the glow. Offer: "ฟังเสียงกลไกเดินเบา ๆ" / "ให้สตูดิโอกลับมาเงียบ".
- **Reading:** `beside`. A short product page whose reader wants to see the watch: it stays lit on the right beside every section, text never covers it (G-frame overlap 0 by construction), the dark set is lit for the product rather than for text, and the spec table reads as a `ledger` on paper in the text column while the watch stays in view. `interleave` would hide the product; `overlay` (the length default) would dim the steel under a quiet zone and lay the table over the watch.
- **Hero object:** procedural, original design: 38 mm 316L case with polished/brushed split and bevel; domed sapphire with AR tint; ripple-stamped dial; twelve domed lume dots and lume hands; knurled screw-down crown; sapphire caseback with lamphu-leaf rotor, beating balance and jewel glints; 20 mm tannin leather strap with quick-release levers.
- **Look:** cinematic-real, studio lighting. CC0: `studio_small_09` (HDRI, reflections and low IBL only), `brown_leather` (strap), `slate_floor` (slab). Post: bloom 0.22 @ 0.9 (lume and speculars only), grain 0.03, vignette 0.22, DOF on the dial (high tier), warmth 0.08. Lens fov 30.

Beauty gates: in `beside` the subject never meets the text (G-frame); the builder still frames each chapter's declared subject (dial, caseback, case, straps, watch) inside the stage with `visible ≥ 0.85`. G-light: every chapter except 2 targets mean 0.11–0.38 with p95 ≥ 0.5 (graded sweep with a real light pool, steel speculars, rim strip never off); only the lights-out interlude carries `tone: night`, and its p95 comes from the lume, the rim edge and large soft bokeh discs. Per-chapter targets are in `chapters[].params.light_target`.

## Signature moments
1. Hold the light on the dial, release, scroll: the studio goes dark and twelve dots and two hands glow yellow-green while the fireflies on the lamphu branch behind pulse the same colour.
2. The flip: the watch turns over on the slab and the lamphu-leaf rotor swings with the reader's scroll.
3. The ending: one warm lamp, the dots still faintly glowing where the page began.

## Guardrails
- **Performance:** high: area-light key, DOF on the dial, 140 firefly sprites, haze, one shadow (key). Mid: no DOF, 80 fireflies, no haze. Low: directional key, no shadows, 40 fireflies, no bloom. Media: 1 HDRI + 2 texture sets at 1k (well under 12 MB). No CC0 model (the watch is an original procedural build).
- **Reduced motion:** no seconds sweep, no firefly pulse, no rotor swing; light changes per chapter are instant; hold-to-charge still works and the glow appears at once.
- **Mobile:** world in the top 52svh, text scrolls below on paper; tap moves the light, long press charges; tags on tap with the live region; DOF off.
- **Chrome:** nothing fixed to the viewport, system cursor untouched, no counting loader, no dots or equalizer bars, no "sound on/off".
- **IP:** `avoid_terms` = [] (original concept, no franchise). No real watch brands, marks, lume trade names, or imitations of existing dials, hands or cases. The content's own fiction notice stays in s08 and the footer repeats it.
